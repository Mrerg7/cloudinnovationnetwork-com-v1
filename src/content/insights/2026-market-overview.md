The global cloud computing market continues its strong expansion in 2026. Multiple analyst firms place the overall market (public + private + hybrid) in the $900 billion to $1.04 trillion range, with public cloud end-user spending alone approaching $850 billion according to Gartner.

Key structural drivers include:

- Sustained capital expenditure by hyperscalers into AI-optimized data centers
- Enterprise migration of remaining core workloads
- Growth of platform and managed services (PaaS)
- Expansion of edge and sovereign cloud offerings

Market share among infrastructure providers remains concentrated, with AWS, Microsoft Azure, and Google Cloud together controlling the majority of global enterprise cloud infrastructure spend.
