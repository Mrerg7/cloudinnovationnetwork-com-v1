Premium .com domains are priced like scarce commercial real estate: location (keywords), frontage (memorability), and zoning (use cases).

## A simple valuation framework

1. **Comparable sales** — check NameBio / DNJournal for similar keyword + length sales in tech and cloud niches.
2. **Keyword strength** — exact-match terms like "cloud" and "innovation" carry enterprise search intent and brand authority.
3. **TLD premium** — .com typically commands 3–10x over alt-TLDs for the same string due to trust and type-in traffic.
4. **Use-case breadth** — media, analyst, consulting, events, education, and community uses widen the buyer pool and support pricing.
5. **Transfer safety** — escrowed, push-based transfers (1–7 days) remove friction and justify premium pricing.

## What buyers pay for

- Instant category authority without years of SEO compounding
- Clean radio-test name for podcasts, events, and sales calls
- Defensive value: owning the category string blocks competitors
